You're here. The money is there. The move is to get one respected person seen with it before you spend on ads.

Make the bag something people hold up, not something they brew.
Let one room you already belong to carry it first, before any ad runs.
Tell the origin through the person who grows it, not the logo.

1. Call the one chef, barista, or host in your circle whose taste others copy. Ask them to serve it, not endorse it.
2. Make one object people want to be photographed with, a bag or cup with no explaining on it.
3. Stop discounting to win shelf space.

Hermes-ready: Win one trusted room first, make the bag worth holding, stop discounting, and let the crowd follow.
