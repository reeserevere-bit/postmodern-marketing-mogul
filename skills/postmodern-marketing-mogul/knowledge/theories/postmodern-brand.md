# Postmodern Brand

[[Postmodern Marketing]]

In response to shifts in [consumer behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_behavior "Consumer behavior") and conditions brought on by [postmodernism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postmodernism "Postmodernism"), many companies changed their [marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing "Marketing")approach to address and create more nimble, immersive experience and customer engagements. **Postmodern branding**are practices of personifying a [brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand "Brand") based on a core set of traits. Postmodern branding behaviors understand and leverage [technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology "Technology"), space, and [mindset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindset "Mindset") of the moment to create an enriched [user experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_experience). Brands have found new ways to enter the home and places in the consumers life. Successful branding within the postmodern society rely more on the developments of brand personality.

Traditional [brand management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_management "Brand management") has grown increasingly hard and complex within the postmodern marketplace.

The significance here is not only focusing on and tracking the organizational created material, but also identifying, monitoring and tracking the material created or suggested by the enthusiast, loyalist, defectors and the like.

Newly developed systems are in need for a brand to be more nimble and personal with their customers. Although this is an ever-evolving time it bears witness that there are new rules to the "new now" or "new new now" of marketing. And although an [organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization "Organization") employs any platforms and processes the brand manager should realize that the brand they are managing is still just a participant in the community they wish to create. With that said, the organization should come to grips with the idea that they may not own the very brand they created

Watching scheduled [TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV "TV") programs, utilizing and interacting with voice recognition services like [Siri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siri "Siri") (behaviors predominantly found in the daily life of the postmodern consumer) are examples of how branded content and utilities are becoming more predominate within our current culture.
