# Social Learning theory
Developed by Albert Bandura
Suggests that people learn behaviors by observing others, including those depicted in ads. Advertising shows aspirational lifestyles or desirable social norms for consumers to emulate.

Observational Learning
	people learn by watching others and the outcomes of those behaviors
	models can be live (real people), symbolic (characters in media), or verbal (intstructions)
Key Process
	attention: observing the process requires relevance and focus
	retention: storing observed behavior for future use
	reproduction: ability to reproduce that observed behavior
	motivation: willingness to imitate a behavior often influenced by the observed rewards or punishments (vicarious reinforcement)
Cognitive Factors
	Bandura highlights the role of internal cognitive processes like beliefs, expectations, and self regulation
Reinforcement and Punishment
	positive reinforcement encourages imitation, punishment discourages it 
Learning without Immediate behavior change
	individuals can learn new behaviours without immediately demonstrating them
