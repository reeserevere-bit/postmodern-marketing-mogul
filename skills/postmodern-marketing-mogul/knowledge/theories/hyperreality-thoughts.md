# Hyperreality thoughts

[[Towel Hyperreality plan]]
Is hyperreality the new reality in 2025. That being real in real life won’t mater and I should position everything for this reality

Hyperreality in 2025,pretty fascinating concept to chew on! Hyperreality, if we’re riffing off Baudrillard, is that state where the lines between reality and simulation blur so much that the simulated feels more real than the physical. Think of it like living in a world where the Instagram filter version of your life outshines the unedited one, and people start caring more about the filter. Is that the new reality now, in February 2025? Let’s unpack it.

On one hand, the digital world’s grip is tighter than ever. Social media, VR, AI-generated content,heck, even the way we work and connect,is increasingly virtual. People are curating their lives online with more polish than a car salesman’s pitch. X posts lately show folks obsessing over virtual influencers, metaverse real estate, and AI-crafted narratives that get more engagement than gritty IRL stuff. The dopamine hit of a perfectly staged TikTok dance seems to trump a messy human moment in the physical world. So yeah, there’s a case that hyperreality’s winning the culture war,perception’s king, and the tangible’s just a means to an end.

But hold up,real life hasn’t checked out yet. People still crave touch, taste, and the chaos of unscripted moments. The physical world’s stubborn; it’s where we eat, sleep, and stub our toes. X chatter shows a backlash too,some are fed up with the polished fakery, pushing for “raw” authenticity (even if that’s just another marketed vibe). Supply chains, climate shifts, and geopolitics still ground us in the concrete. Hyperreality might dominate the mind, but the body’s still tethered to dirt and sweat.

Should you position everything for hyperreality? Depends on your game. If you’re in tech, media, or anything attention-driven, leaning into the simulated, NFTs, virtual experiences, curated personas,could pay off big. The trend’s there: digital assets and online clout are flexing harder than ever. But if you’re in something tactile,food, healthcare, physical goods,don’t ditch the real just yet. People might live in hyperreality for the ‘gram, but they still need to eat and breathe.

The smart move? Hedge your bets. Master the hyperreal,get slick with the digital, the staged, the seductive simulacra,but keep a foot in the messy, meaty real. In 2025, it’s less about picking a side and more about playing both. What’s your angle,business, personal, creative? That’ll tweak the balance. What do you think,am I reading this zeitgeist right, or you seeing something else?
