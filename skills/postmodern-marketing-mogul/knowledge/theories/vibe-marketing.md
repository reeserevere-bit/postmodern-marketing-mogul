Vibe marketing is a new paradigm where AI and automation are used to speed up the entire marketing process,from data analysis to campaign execution and optimization. - Vibe marketing starts with data. You need to understand your audience, trends, and performance metrics quickly.
- **What to Do:** Use AI tools to analyze customer behavior, sales data, trending topics, and social media conversations.
- **Tools to Use:**
