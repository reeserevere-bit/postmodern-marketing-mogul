# Common Knowledge Theory
[[Marketing Theories]]
The **common knowledge theory** in marketing is about communicating a message so effectively that it becomes a widely recognized truth within the target market,where everyone not only knows it but assumes others know it too. Think of how _Ford is the toughest pickup truck_ or _Coca-Cola is the most popular soda_ feels universally accepted.

- **Shared understanding:**
    
    It goes beyond just basic awareness; everyone in the target market needs to not only know the information but also believe that everyone else knows it as well. 
    
- **Coordination and behavior:**
    
    When information is common knowledge, it can lead to predictable consumer behavior and market dynamics, as everyone is operating on the same understanding. 
    
- **Building brand reputation:**
    
    Companies often strive to establish key facts about their brand as common knowledge to build trust and credibility with consumers. 
    
- **Implications for messaging:**
    
    - **Focus on widely accepted truths:** Marketing campaigns should leverage information that is already considered common knowledge within the target market. 
    - **Repetition and consistency:** Repeatedly communicating key messages across various channels can help establish common knowledge. 
    - **Leveraging social proof:** Highlighting how others already know and accept the information can reinforce the common knowledge effect.
