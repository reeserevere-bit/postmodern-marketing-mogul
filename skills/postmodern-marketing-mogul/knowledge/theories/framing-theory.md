# Framing Theory
[[Marketing Theories]]

how rhetorical devices can be used to convince people of a particular point of view

about how you present information to shape perception

two messages can contain the same facts but lead to different responses based on how theyre framed

For marketing, framing is crucial because it influences how consumers interpret a brand, product, or campaign. A well-framed message guides perception without changing the core truth.

## **1. Types of Framing in Marketing**

### **A. Gain vs. Loss Framing**

- **Gain Frame:** Focuses on benefits and positive outcomes.
    - _“Switch to our eco-friendly sneakers and help save 1,000 gallons of water per pair!”_
- **Loss Frame:** Highlights potential negatives or what could be lost.
    - _“Traditional sneakers waste 1,000 gallons of water per pair. Make the switch today!”_
- 🏆 _Best Used For:_ Motivating action,gain frame works for aspirational branding, while loss frame is effective for urgency-driven messaging.

---

### **B. Emotional vs. Rational Framing**

- **Emotional Frame:** Uses feelings and storytelling.
    - _“Imagine your child growing up in a cleaner world,our zero-waste sneakers make that future possible.”_
- **Rational Frame:** Focuses on facts and logical reasoning.
    - _“Our sneakers use 30% less energy to produce, cutting emissions by 40%.”_
- 🏆 _Best Used For:_ Emotional for consumer brands, rational for B2B and high-involvement purchases.

---

### **C. Thematic vs. Episodic Framing**

- **Thematic:** Puts an issue into a larger context, linking it to societal trends.
    - _“The fashion industry is responsible for 10% of global carbon emissions. We’re changing that.”_
- **Episodic:** Focuses on a single instance or personal story.
    - _“Meet Jake. He switched to our sustainable sneakers and reduced his carbon footprint by 40%.”_
- 🏆 _Best Used For:_ Thematic frames build brand purpose, while episodic frames create relatability.

---

### **D. Competitive Framing**

This positions your brand _relative_ to competitors.

- **Comparison Frame:**
    - _“Nike’s sustainable sneakers only use 30% recycled materials. Ours use 100%.”_
- **Contrast Frame:**
    - _“Other brands focus on trends. We focus on sustainability.”_
- 🏆 _Best Used For:_ Differentiating in crowded markets.

---

### **2. How to Use Framing Theory in Message Strategy**

Now, let’s combine this with **two-step flow theory** from earlier.

1️⃣ **Step 1: Brand Creates the Core Message**

- Decide the best frame for your campaign. Example: _Gain frame + emotional frame_
- Craft a message that is clear, compelling, and structured to allow personalization.
    - **Example Core Message:** _“Our sneakers save 1,000 gallons of water per pair,because the planet needs every drop.”_

2️⃣ **Step 2: Opinion Leaders Reframe for Their Audiences**

- Give influencers and thought leaders different ways to frame the message based on their style.
    - A **tech reviewer** might use a rational frame (_“The innovative material reduces waste by 40%”_).
    - A **fitness influencer** might use a gain frame (_“More sustainable, but still built for performance”_).
    - A **fashion blogger** might use an emotional frame (_“Style meets sustainability,because looking good shouldn’t harm the planet”_).

3️⃣ **Step 3: Community Amplification with Guided Framing**

- Encourage users to share their own perspective while keeping the message aligned.
- Use hashtags, challenges, and interactive features to maintain consistency.
    - Example: **#EveryDropCounts Challenge**,users post how they conserve water while wearing the sneakers.

4️⃣ **Step 4: Course Correction If Needed**

- If someone misrepresents the message, gently redirect with clarification.
- Engage in comments to reinforce the preferred framing.

---

### **Real-World Example: Tesla’s Use of Framing Theory**

1️⃣ **Core Message:** Tesla is not just a car, it’s a movement.  
2️⃣ **Framing Strategies:**

- Gain frame: _“Zero emissions = a cleaner planet.”_
- Emotional frame: _“Drive the future.”_
- Competitive frame: _“Faster than a gas car, but fully electric.”_  
    3️⃣ **Amplification:**
- Elon Musk and Tesla enthusiasts reframe the message in ways that appeal to different audiences.
- Some focus on **speed & performance**, others on **tech & innovation**, and some on **saving the planet**,all within Tesla’s original frame.

---

### **Final Takeaway:**

Framing isn’t just about what you say,it’s _how_ you present it so people interpret it the way you want. By pairing **framing theory** with **two-step flow**, you can ensure your message spreads organically _without losing its core meaning_.
