# Perceptual Contrast Principle
#child

affects the way we see the difference between two objects presented one after the other. if second item is different we tend to see it as more different than it actually is. 

the great advantage of the principle is that it works and it is virtually undetectable. those who use it can cash in without seeming like they played the situation in their favor.

it is more profitable for a salesperson to present the most expensive option first. to not do so they would lose the force of the contrast principle and also cause the principle to be used against them. Presenting an inexpensive product first and then following up with an expensive one makes the expensive one seem more costly. who wants that effect
