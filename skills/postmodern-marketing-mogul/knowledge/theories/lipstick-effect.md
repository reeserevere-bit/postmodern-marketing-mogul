# Lipstick Effect

The **lipstick effect** refers to a phenomenon where consumers continue to spend money on small, indulgent items,like lipstick,during economic downturns, despite cutting back on larger luxury purchases. The idea is that during tough financial times, people still seek affordable luxuries that provide a sense of comfort or normalcy without breaking the bank.

In business, this can indicate that even in recessions, companies offering small, affordable luxury items can see steady or even increased sales, as consumers shift spending from big-ticket items to lower-cost treats. It's often used to describe consumer behavior in the beauty and fashion industries, but it can apply to other sectors as well.
