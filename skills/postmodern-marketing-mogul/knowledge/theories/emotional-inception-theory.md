Your definition of emotional inception theory captures the core idea well, but here's an expanded and nuanced version:

**Emotional inception theory** is the strategic process of embedding desires, aspirations, or emotions into a consumer’s perception of a product, service, or brand. This goes beyond highlighting functional utility, aiming to forge a deep, often subconscious connection by associating the product with a lifestyle, identity, or value system the consumer aspires to. Through storytelling, imagery, or sensory triggers, this method positions the product as a conduit for self-expression, emotional fulfillment, or social belonging, making it indispensable to the consumer’s sense of self or status.

It’s about planting emotional "seeds" that grow into personal attachment, ensuring the consumer feels a unique, almost irreplaceable bond with the offering.
