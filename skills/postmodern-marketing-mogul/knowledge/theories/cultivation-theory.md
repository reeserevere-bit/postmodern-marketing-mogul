# Cultivation theory
Suggests that media exposure shapes people's perceptions of reality over time. Advertising reinforces societal norms and expectations, influencing behavior and beliefs.
"people who watch television more are more likely to think that reality is more similar to that whats on tv"

Founded by George Gerbner in 1969 to explain how mass media influences people over time

This is based on the idea that television content is biased and people internalize those biases over time
Suggests media consumption shapes the dominant culture and though processes of society.
