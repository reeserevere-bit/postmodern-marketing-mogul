Focuses on how people create and interpret symbols, like brands or products, to form their identity. Advertising shapes how these symbols gain meaning in social contexts.

These premises are:

1. Humans act toward people or things based on the meanings they assign people or things.
    
2. The meanings we assign to people and things arise from our social interactions with one another. Blumer argued that meaning isn’t inherent in objects but formed through social interactions.
    
3. Humans adjust the meanings they assign people or things by internally interpreting their interactions with the world.
**IN MARKETING**:
In marketing, symbolic interactionism theory suggests that ==consumers assign meanings to products and brands based on their social interactions and interpretations of symbols associated with those products==, essentially buying not just the product itself but the symbolic value it represents within their social group or identity. 

Key points about symbolic interactionism in marketing:

- **Meaning construction:**
    Consumers actively construct meaning about a product through their interactions with it, other people, and the broader cultural context.
- **Symbolic value:**
    Products can symbolize personal attributes, social status, or lifestyle aspirations, influencing purchase decisions.
- **Social interaction:**
    The meanings attached to products are shaped by social interactions and the shared understanding within a group. 
Examples of symbolic interactionism in marketing:
- **Luxury brands:**
    High-end brands often leverage symbols like designer logos to signify status and exclusivity, appealing to consumers seeking to project a certain image.
- **Clothing brands:**
    Certain clothing styles might be associated with specific subcultures or lifestyles, leading consumers to purchase items to identify with a particular group.
- **Advertising campaigns:**
    Marketers often use imagery and messaging that evoke positive emotions or associations with the product, linking it to desired symbolic meanings. 
How marketers can use symbolic interactionism:

- **Understand consumer culture:**
    Analyze the symbolic meanings associated with products within different consumer segments to tailor marketing strategies accordingly.
- **Develop brand identity:**
    Craft a strong brand image with clear symbolic associations that resonate with the target audience.
- **Utilize symbolic messaging:**
    Employ powerful symbols and imagery in advertising to convey the desired meaning and appeal to consumers' self-concepts.
